### objdump switchRuntime (ReleaseFast)

Symbol `vm.Vm.switchRuntime` at `0x1172fc0`, ends `0x11732ae` →
**752 B total** (hot path + cold ensureTotalCapacity growth path).

The 4-field moves ARE visible as SIMD pair moves in the disassembly:

- park (vm → owner): `vmovups 0x9d0(%rdi) → 0xe40(%rax)` (bc_stack, 16 B
  slice ptr+len), `vmovups 0x9e0 → 0xe30` (bc_boxed), `mov 0x9f0 → 0xe10`
  (bc_stack_top), `vmovups 0xa00 + mov 0xa10 → 0xdf8/0xe08`
  (bc_tbc_regs items slice + capacity), then zero-fill of the vm fields
  (`movq $0x8` empty-slice ptr sentinel etc.).
- activate (owner → vm): the exact mirror image + `mov %rsi,0x618(%rdi)`
  (active_runtime_thread = owner).
- Guard head: `mov 0x618(%rdi),%rax; cmp %rsi,%rax; je <ret>` — 3
  instructions, early-out when already on the target thread.
- ensureTotalCapacity hot check: `mov 0xb20(%rsi),%r13; cmp $0x1f,%r13;
  ja <past growth>`.

Per-cycle switchRuntime budget (151 i/cycle self): the 4-field
park+activate move pairs cost 20 i/cycle (10 i/switch × 2), zeroing both
sides ~15 i/cycle, refreshHooksCached 24 i/cycle, guard+prologue/epilogue
~51 i/cycle, ensureTotalCapacity ~0. PUC has NO switchRuntime equivalent
(zero-copy yield: the coroutine's stack IS the lua_State stack,
`luaD_throw`/setjmp longjmps between the two luaV_execute frames) — the
whole 151 i/cycle is architectural transition cost, consistent with the
P16.36 T7 conclusion (transition 99.6% of the gap).

**Verdict**: PUC defers the errored coroutine's TBC `__close` to
`coroutine.close` (luaE_resetthread — the print appears AFTER "calling
coroutine.close(co)..."); luazig runs it DURING the resume error unwind
(print appears BEFORE the resume result line). Root cause (per the
P16.36 Cut 3 audit): PUC `lua_resume`'s unrecoverable-error branch
(ldo.c:989-993) has no `luaD_closeprotected`; luazig's resume error unwind
closes TBC regions eagerly. The normal-return control case is identical
on both engines. Recorded as an open parity item for a dedicated
error-unwind architecture cut — NOT fixed here (this phase's Cut 1 is
table.sort yieldability).
